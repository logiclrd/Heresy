**19 zero-underrun stationary trials, 8 sustained-overload stationary trials.** In all 19 zero-underrun windows, the minimum **sampled** ring occupancy was 1,024 frames; in all eight failing windows it was **zero**. Minimum sampled occupancy is not an instantaneous hardware-buffer guarantee. The `3x7` window, despite **zero** underruns, had only **0.151 ms (2.8%) mean time margin** to the producer's 5.333-ms block budget and **8.5%** over-budget blocks. **`3x7` is particularly marginal**, not a suggested reliable operating limit. Other last-zero windows also have limited mean headroom: `2x9` 0.602 ms, `4x5` 0.579 ms, `7x3` 0.489 ms, `1x15` 0.798 ms. Do not turn these observed short-window successes into guarantees or a recommended default cap without margin.

### Full-session and off-interval accounting

The complete **691.5-second** SDL session accumulated **6,110 ring underrun events**, **30,435 over-budget producer blocks / 122,789 PCM blocks**, **31,433,984 PCM frames**, **39 completed Multi worker expansions / 78 prepared constituents**, **zero worker cancellations**, worker peak waiting queue **5**, accumulated worker preparation time **1.740 ms**, producer peak block **69.681 ms** (a *whole-session peak*, not assigned to a particular load), callback processing peak **2.996 ms**, **zero** SDL callbacks counted beyond the *nominal stream-request duration*, and no nonempty CSV fault types. Individual marked windows had **no new worker-completion/child-preparation counts**: they were measuring **stationary loads after settling**, not worker preparation throughput.

The **eight labeled failing windows contain 3,006 additional underrun events**. **3,104 of the 6,110 whole-session events occurred outside the measured first/last-sample trial windows**, including unmarked configuration changes/settling, startup and marker-boundary gaps. **Do not misattribute those 3,104 events to a specific labeled configuration**; Heresy's ring stayed active during transitions between marked trials. A high whole-session total does not contradict the many zero-increment stationary intervals.

**Root-cause classification for this run:** At all eight failing stationary loads, the PCM producer spent most or all blocks **over its production budget**, the ring was sampled empty, and the output-short-read counter increased. The separate Multi preparation worker **did not** run work during the measured steady intervals, had no recorded failures or cancellations, and was not the sustained bottleneck. The small SDL callback wall time does not demonstrate an OS device deadline guarantee; the failure was an **underproducing PCM pipeline** at those sustained DSP loads.

## Bounded C5(f) acceptance and remaining risks

**C5(f) FIRST PHYSICAL ENVELOPE MATRIX: ACCEPTED, narrowly**, for observed per-note-voice count brackets and the user's identification of continuously disruptive loads on this machine and one graph. This is enough to distinguish supported-in-this-short-trial candidates from definite tested unsupported configurations **without requiring the tester to deliberately repeat every already-demonstrated overload**. It is **not** a global safe-voice-count standard, long-duration soak test, power-state worst-case guarantee or C6 full-editor listening acceptance. Borderline zero-underrun configurations should be treated as **headroom warnings**, not recommended reliable limits. If future work targets capacity optimization, remeasure the same graph and backend before/after. No source algorithm fix is indicated by the current data: **same-note recovery** was previously verified, and the present sustained failure mode is producer throughput at high load.
